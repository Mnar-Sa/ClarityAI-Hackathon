**Project Overview**

***MindMap*** is an AI-powered wellbeing support solution developed for the IBM Hackathon.

Built on IBM watsonx Orchestrate, the project demonstrates how agentic AI can support early emotional wellbeing through structured orchestration, explainable insights, and responsible AI governance.



**Problem Statement**

Many teenagers experience emotional distress without early visibility or timely support.

***MindMap*** addresses this gap by identifying early emotional risk signals and enabling proactive, ethical, and privacy-preserving support.



**Solution Overview**

***MindMap*** uses an agent-based architecture to analyze emotional signals, assess risk levels, and trigger appropriate responses while ensuring explainability and ethical AI practices.



**Key components include:**

Intake Agent for user interaction

Emotion & Sentiment Analysis using watsonx.ai

Risk Scoring Engine

Notification & Support Agent

Governance and explainability via watsonx.governance



**Architecture & IBM watsonx Integration:**

The solution is built using IBM watsonx services:

1. &#x20;watsonx.ai for sentiment and emotion analysis
2. &#x20;watsonx Orchestrate for agent coordination and workflow execution
3. &#x20;watsonx.data for handling structured mock datasets
4. &#x20;watsonx.governance for responsible AI, bias monitoring, and explainability



(Refer to the Architecture Diagram and MindMap asset in project files.)



**Preview & Internal Validation:**

The solution was previewed internally using simulated conversations and mock emotional data.

Mock datasets were used to validate:

* End-to-end agent flow
* Emotional signal extraction
* Risk score generation
* Tone sensitivity and privacy boundaries

This preview ensured the conceptual readiness of the solution before deployment.



**Deployment Strategy:**

***MindMap*** is designed to be deployed using IBM watsonx Orchestrate as a conversational AI agent.

The solution supports a phased rollout approach, starting with limited access for monitoring quality, performance, and emotional safety.

Future deployments may include web-based chat interfaces and integration with messaging platforms.

Governance mechanisms ensure compliance, transparency, and ethical AI usage throughout deployment.



**Social Impact**

***MindMap*** aims to:

* Enable early emotional support
* Reduce escalation of mental health risks
* Promote ethical and responsible AI in youth wellbeing
* Support schools and communities with proactive insights
